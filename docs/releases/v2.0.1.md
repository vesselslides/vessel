A safer presenter remote, with a lock, and cleaner icons.

## Download

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Mac with an Apple chip (M1 and later) | `Vessel-2.0.1-mac-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel chip | `Vessel-2.0.1-mac-x64.dmg` |
| Windows (any) | `Vessel-2.0.1-win.exe` |
| Windows, Intel or AMD only (smaller) | `Vessel-2.0.1-win-x64.exe` |

Already have Vessel? Click **Update available** in the toolbar, or choose
**Check for Updates…** (the Vessel menu on a Mac, Help on Windows), then
**Download and Install**. Your songs, playlists and settings stay as they are.

Vessel isn't code-signed yet, so a new install needs one extra step. On a Mac:
**System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Open Anyway**. On Windows: **More
info ▸ Run anyway**. The [README](https://github.com/vesselslides/vessel#download-and-install)
walks through it.

## What's new

### Presenter remote

- **Only their items.** Right-click an item in the service ▸ **For the
  presenter** (saved with the playlist). With any marked, the presenter's
  phone or iPad shows and moves only those, and its Next and Back wait while
  anything else is on screen.
- **Lock.** The Live panel has a **Presenter remote** section (while the
  remote is on) showing which items are theirs, and a **Locked** switch: the
  presenter can still read their slides and notes, but their buttons do
  nothing. The iPad says why its buttons are waiting or locked.

### Fixed

- The presenter's Next no longer moves a song that's on screen after their
  presentation, and Back no longer puts words back after you clear them or
  black out the screen.

### Icons

- Line icons replace emoji and symbols throughout: the playlist buttons,
  slide badges, library folders, the media bin, and Back and Next on the phone
  and iPad remotes.
