Vessel 1.0: style your words in Edit, soften your backgrounds, and a roomier
media bin.

## Download

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Mac with an Apple chip (M1 and later) | `Vessel-1.0.0-mac-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel chip | `Vessel-1.0.0-mac-x64.dmg` |
| Windows (any) | `Vessel-1.0.0-win.exe` |
| Windows, Intel or AMD only (smaller) | `Vessel-1.0.0-win-x64.exe` |

Already have Vessel? Click **Update available** in the toolbar, or choose
**Check for Updates…** (the Vessel menu on a Mac, Help on Windows), then
**Download and Install**. Your songs, playlists and settings stay as they are.

Vessel isn't code-signed yet, so a new install needs one extra step. On a Mac:
**System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Open Anyway**. On Windows: **More
info ▸ Run anyway**. The [README](https://github.com/vesselslides/vessel#download-and-install)
walks through it.

## What's new

- **Text styling in Edit.** A new **Text** bar changes the font, size,
  bold, italic, colour and alignment of every slide at once, or of one group
  (just the chorus, say). A− and A+ keep a title bigger than the words under
  it.
- **Background look.** Right-click a picture or video in the media bin ▸
  **Adjust look** to blur it, darken it or take the colour out, with a
  preview. It looks that way wherever it's a background: the screens, the
  stream, NDI® and the slide thumbnails.
- **Start with an empty slide.** **New song** can now be created without any
  words: you get one blank slide, opened in Edit, ready for announcements or
  sermon points.
- **A roomier media bin.** Media fills the whole bin as a grid that scrolls
  down, instead of one long row. Drag the bin's top edge to see more.
- **Easier dragging into the service.** Drag a presentation's title in Show,
  or a file under **Open files**, into the service. With no playlist open,
  dropping a song on the Service area starts a new one.

## Fixed

- **PowerPoint slides with a title and body.** In Edit, each text box now
  gets its own field. Before, saving a change put every box's words into the
  first box.
