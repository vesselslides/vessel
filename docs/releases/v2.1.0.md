Presentations look right even when this computer is missing their fonts,
and chord sheets keep chords written like "A-D".

## Download

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Mac with an Apple chip (M1 and later) | `Vessel-2.1.0-mac-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel chip | `Vessel-2.1.0-mac-x64.dmg` |
| Windows (any) | `Vessel-2.1.0-win.exe` |
| Windows, Intel or AMD only (smaller) | `Vessel-2.1.0-win-x64.exe` |

Already have Vessel? Click **Update available** in the toolbar, or choose
**Check for Updates…** (the Vessel menu on a Mac, Help on Windows), then
**Download and Install**. Your songs, playlists and settings stay as they are.

Vessel isn't code-signed yet, so a new install needs one extra step. On a Mac:
**System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Open Anyway**. On Windows: **More
info ▸ Run anyway**. The [README](https://github.com/vesselslides/vessel#download-and-install)
walks through it.

## What's new

### Missing fonts

- **Fonts that come with the file.** A PowerPoint that carries its fonts
  (PowerPoint's *Embed fonts*, and Canva exports) brings them into Vessel's
  own fonts folder, `Documents/Vessel/Fonts`. Every screen uses them on a
  computer that doesn't have them; an installed copy always comes first.
- **The Fonts window** opens after an import that needs it, and a *fonts
  missing* notice sits beside the presentation's name in Show and Edit. For
  each missing font:
  - **Download (free)** for free fonts, from Google Fonts.
  - **Choose font file…** for a font you own (.ttf or .otf).
  - **Use instead**, with the closest free look-alike suggested first
    (Gotham → Montserrat, Proxima Nova → Figtree) and a before-and-after
    preview.
- **Just on this computer, or in the song.** A stand-in can apply on this
  computer only (the song stays as it is, so a computer with the real font
  still uses it) or be written into the song.
- **Fits its box.** Slideshow text drawn with another font shrinks to fit
  instead of spilling over.
- **Travels with your songs.** Vessel files and playlist bundles carry the
  fonts their songs use. Share only fonts your licence allows.

### Fixed

- Chord sheets: chords joined by a dash, like `A-D`, are read as two chords.
  Before, the whole chord line was taken for words and its chords were lost.
  Import the song again to bring its chords in.
