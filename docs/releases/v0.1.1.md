Vessel now wears its own colours: the **Vessel Slides** navy and gold, from
the moment it opens.

## Download

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Mac with an Apple chip (M1 and later) | `Vessel-0.1.1-mac-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel chip | `Vessel-0.1.1-mac-x64.dmg` |
| Windows (any) | `Vessel-0.1.1-win.exe` |
| Windows, Intel or AMD only (smaller) | `Vessel-0.1.1-win-x64.exe` |

Already have Vessel 0.1.0? It offers this update within a day; choose
**Download and Install**. Your songs, playlists and settings stay as they are.

Vessel isn't code-signed yet, so a new install needs one extra step. On a Mac:
**System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Open Anyway**. On Windows: **More
info ▸ Run anyway**. The [README](https://github.com/vesselslides/vessel#download-and-install)
walks through it.

## What's new

- **A splash while Vessel starts:** the logo is pressed like a play button and
  the name slides out beside it, then it fades into the main window. Turn it
  off in **Preferences ▸ General ▸ Starting up**.
- **The logo on the welcome window,** on a navy banner.
- **Brand colours throughout:** dark mode is now navy with soft gold, and light
  mode is ivory with bronze. The phone remote matches. The red LIVE marker and
  your section colours are unchanged, and nothing changes on the audience
  screen.
