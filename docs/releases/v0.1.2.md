A remote for the person speaking, notes on every slide, and lyrics that
reach OBS on Windows.

Already have Vessel? It offers this update within a day; choose **Download
and Install**. Your songs, playlists and settings stay as they are.

Vessel isn't code-signed yet, so a new install needs one extra step. On a Mac:
**System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Open Anyway**. On Windows: **More
info ▸ Run anyway**. The [README](https://github.com/vesselslides/vessel#download-and-install)
walks through it.

## What's new

- **Presenter remote.** The Remote window now has a second PIN for the
  preacher or presenter. On their phone or tablet they see the slide on
  screen and the next one, their notes for that slide in large print, a clock
  and how long they've been speaking, and Black, Back and Next. Tap a slide to
  jump to it. Presenters can't clear the screen or change the service, and
  Next stops at their last slide instead of moving on into the next song.
  Turn the phone sideways, or use an iPad, for slides and notes side by side.
- **Notes on every slide.** Importing a PowerPoint brings its speaker notes
  across, and in **Edit** there's a notes box under each slide. Notes are kept
  where ProPresenter keeps them, so they travel with the file.

## Fixed

- **OBS on Windows showed only green.** When OBS covered Vessel's stream
  output window, Windows stopped drawing it, so OBS kept showing the first,
  empty frame. The stream, audience, stage and NDI® outputs now keep drawing
  when they're covered or minimised.
