Vessel 2.0: play PowerPoint, Keynote and Google Slides as they were made,
animate your slides, choose many slides at once, and organise the library in
folders.

## Download

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Mac with an Apple chip (M1 and later) | `Vessel-2.0.0-mac-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel chip | `Vessel-2.0.0-mac-x64.dmg` |
| Windows (any) | `Vessel-2.0.0-win.exe` |
| Windows, Intel or AMD only (smaller) | `Vessel-2.0.0-win-x64.exe` |

Already have Vessel? Click **Update available** in the toolbar, or choose
**Check for Updates…** (the Vessel menu on a Mac, Help on Windows), then
**Download and Install**. Your songs, playlists and settings stay as they are.

Vessel isn't code-signed yet, so a new install needs one extra step. On a Mac:
**System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Open Anyway**. On Windows: **More
info ▸ Run anyway**. The [README](https://github.com/vesselslides/vessel#download-and-install)
walks through it.

## What's new

### Slideshows, as they were made

- **Import PowerPoint (.pptx and .ppt), Keynote (.key) and Google Slides** (as
  .pptx) as a *Slideshow*: every slide as it looks, with its click animations
  and speaker notes. The Import window still offers *Vessel slides* (to edit
  here) and *Only the words*.
- **Click by click.** → and Space bring in the next point, picture or line;
  ← takes it away. Appear, fade, fly in, float in, wipe and zoom play, in and
  out, with "with previous", "after previous" and delays. "Move on after 5
  seconds" becomes the slide's timer.
- **Stage screen:** the slide now, the next click or slide, and the speaker
  notes in big type. **Presenter remote:** a picture of each click, with the
  notes.
- Slides keep their fonts, line and letter spacing, SVG artwork (Canva) and
  transparency.

### Animations

- **10 slide transitions:** fade, push, slide over, zoom, blur, flip, cube,
  wipe, iris and cut.
- **16 ways for the words to come in:** fade, fade up, typewriter, pop,
  bounce, blur in, drop in, rise, slide in, zoom in, zoom out, flip up, spin,
  glow, wave and spread in, **by** everything at once, line, sentence, word or
  letter. Slow, Normal or Fast.
- Set them in Edit's **Animation** bar (with ▶ Preview), or right-click a
  slide in Show or Edit. They play on the screens and the stream.

### Choose many slides

- **⌘-click / Ctrl-click, Shift-click and ⌘A / Ctrl+A** in Show and Edit.
- Edit's **Text** bar now changes only the chosen slides, and adds **Top /
  Middle / Bottom**, with icons.
- **Right-click a slide** in Show for everything at once: transition, words,
  text (size, bold, italic, alignment, position, colour), background and its
  fit, pictures, look, timer, turn off and delete.

### Pictures and videos

- **Adjust look** now has brightness, contrast, saturation, hue, warmth,
  blur, darken and vignette. Keep a look on the file everywhere, or for chosen
  slides only (right-click a slide ▸ *Adjust look…*).
- **Fixed:** blur and darken now show on the screens for pictures placed on
  slides, not only for backgrounds.
- **Fill, Fit or Stretch** from the media bin's right-click (for that file)
  or a slide's *Background* menu (for chosen slides).

### Library folders

- **Folders inside folders** in the song library: right-click for *New
  folder*, *Rename* and *Move to Trash*; drag songs and folders onto a folder,
  or right-click a song ▸ *Move to folder*. They're real folders, so Finder,
  Explorer and backups see the same thing.

Transitions, word animations and slide-only looks are saved in the song file
as Vessel's own; ProPresenter opens these files but doesn't play them.
