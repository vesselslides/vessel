Chords for the band: keep them from your chord sheets, transpose in a click,
and see them on the stage screen, chord charts and the presenter remote.

## Download

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Mac with an Apple chip (M1 and later) | `Vessel-1.1.0-mac-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel chip | `Vessel-1.1.0-mac-x64.dmg` |
| Windows (any) | `Vessel-1.1.0-win.exe` |
| Windows, Intel or AMD only (smaller) | `Vessel-1.1.0-win-x64.exe` |

Already have Vessel? Click **Update available** in the toolbar, or choose
**Check for Updates…** (the Vessel menu on a Mac, Help on Windows), then
**Download and Install**. Your songs, playlists and settings stay as they are.

Vessel isn't code-signed yet, so a new install needs one extra step. On a Mac:
**System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Open Anyway**. On Windows: **More
info ▸ Run anyway**. The [README](https://github.com/vesselslides/vessel#download-and-install)
walks through it.

## What's new: chords

Chords are for the band. They never show on the audience screen, the stream
or NDI®.

- **Paste or import a chord sheet.** Paste one into **New song** or **Edit
  lyrics as text**, with chords above the words or in brackets
  (`[G]Amazing`), or import a `.txt` or ChordPro file. The audience sees only
  the words; the chords stay on the words they're played over. Section labels
  like `Verse 1:` become groups and the key is worked out for you.
- **Edit chords.** In Edit, the **Chords** button shows each slide's chords in
  brackets to add, move or change. **Edit lyrics as text** shows the whole
  song with chords above the words or in brackets.
- **Transpose anywhere.** One **Key** button in Edit, Show and the Live
  panel's Stage section: step a half step with ♭ and ♯, pick any key, or go
  back to the key it's written in. Nothing is lost going back.
- **On the stage screen.** Chords sit above the words, with the key. Live ▸
  Stage turns them off, and *Adjust…* chooses letters or Nashville numbers
  (1 4 5), a capo, and the chords' size and colour.
- **Chord charts.** File ▸ Export ▸ **Chord chart** makes a PDF for the band,
  in the key each song is played in.
- **Presenter remote.** Songs with chords get **Notes** and **Chords** tabs on
  the phone or iPad, for a worship leader.

Chords and the key are saved in the song file where ProPresenter keeps them.
Import ▸ *Chords* (and Preferences ▸ Import) can still leave chords out, or
show them as words.
