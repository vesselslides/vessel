The first public release of **Vessel**: free presentation software for
churches, for Mac and Windows.

## Download

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Mac with an Apple chip (M1 and later) | `Vessel-0.1.0-mac-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel chip | `Vessel-0.1.0-mac-x64.dmg` |
| Windows (any) | `Vessel-0.1.0-win.exe` |
| Windows, Intel or AMD only (smaller) | `Vessel-0.1.0-win-x64.exe` |

Vessel isn't code-signed yet, so the first start needs one extra step. On a Mac:
**System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Open Anyway**. On Windows: **More
info ▸ Run anyway**. The [README](https://github.com/vesselslides/vessel#download-and-install)
walks through it.

## What's in it

- **Songs** with groups, arrangements, themes and motion or still backgrounds.
  Type them in, or import PowerPoint, Word, text, ChordPro, Planning Center and
  ProPresenter files (versions 5, 6 and 7).
- **The Bible:** 30 free translations in 17 languages, offline once downloaded.
- **Service playlists** with headings; → and Space roll from one song into the next.
- **Screens:** the audience screen, a stage display, and a stream output with
  lyrics for OBS or vMix, also over NDI®.
- **Media:** pictures, motion backgrounds and audio in folders; drag them onto
  slides as backgrounds or new slides.
- **Messages, lower thirds, announcement loops**, slide timers, a logo button
  and a black screen.
- **Control** from a phone, a Stream Deck, Companion or a MIDI pad.
- **Sharing:** `.vessel` files carry a service with its songs and media to
  another church; a theme gallery; export to PDF (slides or lyrics sheet),
  pictures, plain text, or a playlist with all its media.
- **Peace of mind:** saves as you go, screens that reopen by themselves, backups
  in one file, and a practice mode that keeps the real screens dark.
- **CCLI** copyright lines and usage reports.

The first start sets up a **Sample Sunday Service** with hymns, a reading, a
sermon, motion backgrounds and audio, and the **?** button runs a short tour.

## Good to know

- Vessel checks for updates once a day (Preferences ▸ General) and asks before
  downloading anything.
- No accounts, analytics or tracking. See [PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/vesselslides/vessel/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).
- Found a problem? **Help ▸ Report a Problem…** saves a file with Vessel's logs;
  [open an issue](https://github.com/vesselslides/vessel/issues/new/choose) and attach it.
