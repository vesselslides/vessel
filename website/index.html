What it does

Everything Sunday needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Built for the volunteer who has ten minutes before the service starts, and for the one who runs it every week.

Songs

Verses, choruses and arrangements with themes and motion backgrounds. Type them in, or bring them from PowerPoint, Word, ChordPro or Planning Center.

The Bible

30 free translations in 17 languages, offline once downloaded. Type "John 3:16" and click a verse to show it.

Service playlists

The running order for the day, with headings. Space rolls from one song straight into the next.

Media

Pictures, motion backgrounds with play and scrub controls, and audio, kept in folders.

Messages and lower thirds

Over the slides: "We begin in 4:59", or "Pastor Sam, Lead Pastor" under the speaker.

Announcement loops

Slide timers that run on their own before the service, a logo button and a black screen.

CCLI built in

Copyright lines on every song and usage reports ready when it's time to send them.

Share with other churches

One .vessel file carries a whole service with its songs, themes and media. Export PDFs, pictures and lyric sheets too.

Peace of mind

Saves as you go, screens reopen by themselves, one-file backups, and a practice mode that keeps the real screens dark.

Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. Vessel is free software under the GNU GPL v3. There's nothing to unlock and nothing to subscribe to. If it helps your church, you're welcome to give something back, but you never have to.

Will it open our ProPresenter library?

Vessel opens and saves ProPresenter 7 presentations and playlists, and imports versions 5 and 6, so you can bring your songs across and keep using them.

What computer do I need?

A Mac with macOS 12 or later (Apple chip or Intel), or a PC with Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit or Arm). Add a second screen for the audience and a third for a stage display if you have one.

Why does my computer warn me when I install it?

Vessel isn't code-signed yet, which costs money every year. The first time you open it, macOS or Windows asks you to confirm. The download page shows the one extra click.

Does it work without the internet?

Yes. Vessel only goes online for update checks, Bible downloads, the theme gallery and Planning Center, and only when you use them.

Where do I get help?

Inside Vessel, Help has a tour and a practice song. For problems, ideas and questions, open an issue on GitHub; there's a form for each.

Support

Free to use. Kept going by gifts.

There's no company behind Vessel selling upgrades. Gifts pay for code signing, so computers stop warning about it, for testing hardware, hosting and time.
