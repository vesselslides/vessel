Install

One extra click, the first time.

Vessel isn't code-signed yet, so your computer asks you to confirm before it opens it. You only do this once.

  1. Open the .dmg and drag Vessel into Applications.
  2. Open Vessel from Applications. The first time, macOS may say it can't check the app for malicious software.
  3. Open System SettingsPrivacy & Security, scroll down to the message about Vessel and choose Open Anyway, then Open.
Not sure which Mac you have? Choose Apple menuAbout This Mac. "Chip" means an Apple chip; "Processor … Intel" means Intel.
  1. Run the .exe you downloaded.
  2. If "Windows protected your PC" appears, choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Vessel installs for you (no administrator needed) and opens.
The standard installer works on every PC and picks the right version. The smaller one is for Intel and AMD PCs only.
